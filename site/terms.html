A family account must be created by a parent or legal guardian who is at least 18 years old. The parent is responsible for the family account and for how their children use the app. Children may use ADROS only after a parent has added them to the family. A child should not create a family account.

Keep your password and your family's join codes private. Anyone with a join code can join your family on their device, so only share codes with your own family. You can remove a device or delete the whole family account at any time in Settings. Tell us straight away at adros.helper@gmail.com if you think someone got into your account without permission.

Everything your family puts into ADROS (plans, notes, photos, school files, answers) stays yours. You give us permission to store it, copy it between your family's devices and process it only to run the app for you, as explained in the Privacy policy. Only upload things you have the right to use, such as your own photos and your children's school work for personal study. Do not upload anything illegal, harmful or someone else's private information.

5. The AI study helper

ADROS can make worksheets, explanations and checks with artificial intelligence (AI). A parent must turn this on first.

  • AI answers can be wrong or incomplete. They are a study aid, not a replacement for teachers, school books or a parent's judgement.
  • A parent should check important answers and marks, especially before tests.
  • Use the Report button on any answer that is wrong or not right for a child. We use reports to improve the helper.
  • Each plan has a monthly number of AI answers. We may slow down or stop AI use that looks like misuse.
